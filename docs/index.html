FinSTOD volume cubes

Drag to rotate. The field is painted on the six faces of the sampled cube (log(1+s), 2–98 stretch). Native grids are coarse (ABC 120³, Lorenz-96 100³).

ABC flow — full phase space (x, y, z), m = 3, τ = 20.

Lorenz-96 — (y1, y2, y3) of m = 6 with the other coordinates frozen, τ = 4.
